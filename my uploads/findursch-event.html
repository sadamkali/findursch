About the event

Sample Academy invites you to a full-day open day. Walk through the classrooms, laboratories, library and sports grounds with our teachers and student guides, and see what a normal school day looks like.

Staff will be available to talk about admissions, fees, curriculum and boarding. Bring your child, your questions and any documents you would like to discuss.
