Education leaders call for stronger school health programmes

Photo: Source name

Education leaders say stronger health programmes in schools would help children learn better, and have called for closer cooperation between schools, clinics and local governments.

ND
FindURSCH News DeskSource name · Kampala
17 Nov 202610 views
SHARE:FacebookWhatsAppTwitter

Education leaders have issued a joint appeal for stronger health support inside schools, saying that regular screening, clean water and trained school nurses make a measurable difference to attendance and results.

According to the organisers, schools, clinics and local governments need to share information more openly, so that a child who falls ill is followed up quickly and parents are told what help is available.

By closing these gaps, the group says, schools can protect children's wellbeing and give every learner a fair chance to succeed in class.
